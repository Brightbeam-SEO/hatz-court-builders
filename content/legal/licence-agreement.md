## 1. About Hatz Court Builders

Hatz Court Builders is a sports court construction contractor serving residential and commercial customers across Idaho and Arizona.

Our team works with acrylic surfaces, cushioned court systems, modular sports flooring, synthetic turf, asphalt, and concrete.
