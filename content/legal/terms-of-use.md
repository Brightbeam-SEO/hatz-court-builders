## 1. About Hatz Court Builders

Hatz Court Builders is a sports court construction contractor established in 2024, serving residential and commercial customers across Idaho and Arizona.

Our two primary service markets are Boise, Idaho, and Scottsdale, Arizona. We operate as a service-area business, providing construction services at customers' properties and project locations.

We specialize in custom tennis court construction, basketball court construction, pickleball court installation, volleyball courts, futsal courts, bocce courts, padel courts, and multi-use sports facilities.

Our services also include court resurfacing, repairs, renovations, custom court designs, surface installation, striping, lighting, fencing, and site preparation.
