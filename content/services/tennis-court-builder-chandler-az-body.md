## What Chandler Properties Need for a Well-Built Tennis Court

Tennis courts require more planning than simply adding a paved area. Ground conditions, surface materials, drainage, and space around the playing area can influence how the court functions over time.

Property owners in Chandler may encounter several challenges when building or improving a tennis court.

- High outdoor temperatures: Chandler's summer heat makes surface selection and court conditions important parts of planning an outdoor playing area.
- Uneven or unstable ground: Areas with slopes or poor base conditions may need additional site preparation before construction.
- Aging court surfaces: Existing tennis courts may develop cracks, worn coatings, or fading markings after years of use.
- Limited property dimensions: Residential lots may require careful planning to accommodate the court and surrounding playing space.
- Outdated court features: Older recreational facilities may need updated fencing, lighting, or playing surfaces.

## Selecting a Tennis Court Surface for Chandler's Climate

The surface you choose affects the feel of the court, the way the ball responds, and the type of upkeep it may need.

For outdoor tennis court construction in Chandler, acrylic systems are a common choice. Standard acrylic creates a firm playing surface, while cushioned acrylic systems add layers that change the feel underfoot.

Modular surfaces provide another option for certain recreational court projects. Concrete and asphalt are commonly used as structural bases beneath finished court coatings.

Chandler's dry climate, strong sunlight, and high summer temperatures are also worth discussing when selecting materials and planning long-term court care.

Hatz Court Builders can help you compare surface options based on your intended use, property conditions, and project goals.
