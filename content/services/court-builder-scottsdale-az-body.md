## Maintaining Athletic Courts in Scottsdale's Desert Environment

Proper court care helps preserve the playing surface and identify developing concerns before more substantial repairs become necessary.

Maintenance requirements vary according to the surface system, expected usage, and environmental exposure.

### Remove Dust and Windblown Debris

Scottsdale's dry conditions can result in dust, leaves, and other debris accumulating on outdoor courts.

Cleaning should follow the recommendations for the installed material.

Certain aggressive cleaning methods may damage athletic coatings, modular flooring, or specialized surface finishes.

### Monitor Surface Wear From Sun Exposure

Outdoor acrylic surfaces may gradually lose color and texture through UV exposure and regular play.

Property owners should watch for fading, peeling, exposed pavement, worn markings, and changes in surface traction.

These conditions may indicate that repairs or resurfacing should be considered.

### Inspect Drainage Following Monsoon Rainfall

Heavy seasonal rainfall can reveal low spots, perimeter erosion, and other drainage concerns.

Checking the court after storms can help identify areas where water repeatedly accumulates or surrounding materials have shifted.

### Check Court Equipment and Perimeter Features

Basketball hoops, net posts, fence gates, lighting components, and other equipment should be inspected periodically.

Loose or damaged components can affect the functionality of the recreational area.

### Maintain Modular and Synthetic Surface Systems

Modular flooring and synthetic turf may require different maintenance methods from acrylic hardcourts.

Cleaning, component inspections, and other care should follow the manufacturer's recommendations.

### Review the Court Before Peak Recreational Seasons

Scottsdale's milder months may bring increased outdoor court use.

Inspecting the surface, equipment, markings, and drainage before periods of heavier activity can help identify improvements that need attention.

Routine maintenance cannot prevent every type of deterioration, but it can support the long-term condition of the court.
