Players and facility managers in Boise, Meridian, Scottsdale, and Phoenix choose cushioning when comfort and return play matter as much as color and ball response.

## Who Cushioned Court Systems Are For

Cushioned systems are a strong fit for recreational athletes, clubs, HOAs, and schools that want a softer landing without leaving the hard-court game. Private homeowners who play frequently often request cushioning to reduce joint fatigue. Commercial facilities use it to improve player experience and keep courts booked.

Idaho and Arizona climates still require proper base prep and coating windows — cushion layers do not replace drainage or structural repairs.

## When Cushioning (or a Full Refresh) Should Move Quickly

Hard courts that feel punishing, produce player complaints, or show worn acrylic over an aging pad often need more than a light color coat. If your club is losing players to comfort issues, or an HOA amenity court is underused because it feels harsh, a cushioned court system can change how the court plays.

When the base is cracked or holding water, we address structure first. Cushion and acrylic over a failing pad will not deliver lasting comfort.

## Common Problems Cushioned Court Systems Address

Property owners pursue cushioned systems when they notice:

- Player fatigue or joint discomfort on standard hard courts
- Feedback that the court “feels too hard” compared with club standards
- Aging acrylic that needs a full system refresh, not just paint
- A desire for comfort without switching to a completely different surface type
- Multi-age play where kids, adults, and seniors share the same court
- Clubs competing for members who expect a more premium court feel

These pain points are specific to cushioned court systems and hard-court comfort — not generic resurfacing fluff.

## What’s Included in a Cushioned Court System

A cushioned project may include:

- Base evaluation and necessary repairs
- Surface cleaning and preparation
- Installation of cushion layers
- Acrylic color and texture coats over the cushion
- Sport-specific or multi-sport striping
- Color selection for residential or branded commercial courts
- Comparison guidance if [modular tiles](/modular-court-surfaces/) or [synthetic courts](/synthetic-courts/) would suit the sport better

Cushion thickness and layer counts can vary. We match the system to how intensely the court will be used.

## Why Hire Professionals for Cushioned Court Systems

Cushion products must be installed to manufacturer standards for thickness, curing, and adhesion. Shortcuts create soft spots, delamination, or inconsistent bounce. Applying cushion over an unrepaired base wastes the upgrade.

Professional installation protects the premium materials you are paying for and keeps play characteristics predictable. Local experience helps schedule work around weather in both of our primary service regions.
