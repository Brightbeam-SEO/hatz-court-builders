## Maintaining Your Athletic Court in Mesa's Desert Climate

Regular maintenance can help preserve the appearance and playing characteristics of an athletic court.

The appropriate maintenance method depends on the installed surface, usage frequency, and outdoor exposure.

### Remove Dust and Windblown Debris

Mesa's dry conditions can result in dust, leaves, and other debris accumulating on recreational surfaces.

Routine cleaning with suitable equipment helps maintain a more consistent playing area.

Cleaning methods should follow the recommendations for the installed material.

### Inspect the Court After Monsoon Storms

Heavy seasonal rainfall can reveal drainage concerns that may not be visible during dry weather.

Look for recurring puddles, perimeter erosion, displaced materials, and debris carried onto the court.

Persistent water accumulation should be evaluated before it contributes to additional deterioration.

### Monitor Acrylic Surface Wear

Outdoor acrylic coatings may gradually fade or wear through regular use and continued sunlight exposure.

Peeling coatings, exposed pavement, faded markings, and changes in traction may indicate that resurfacing should be considered.

### Check Modular Flooring Components

Modular flooring should be inspected for damaged tiles, loose connections, irregular movement, and accumulated debris.

Maintenance should follow the manufacturer's requirements, particularly when the surface experiences substantial temperature changes.

### Maintain Equipment and Court Fencing

Basketball hoops, net posts, gates, fencing, and lighting components should be checked periodically for damage or movement.

Addressing loose or deteriorated components can help preserve the functionality of the recreational area.

### Review the Court Before Periods of Heavy Use

Many outdoor recreational facilities experience increased activity during Arizona's milder months.

Inspecting the playing surface, markings, drainage, and equipment before heavier usage can help identify repairs that may be needed.

Proper maintenance cannot prevent every type of deterioration, but it can help support the long-term condition of your Mesa athletic court.
