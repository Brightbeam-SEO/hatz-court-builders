## Planning for Meridian's Soil, Weather, and Property Conditions

The quality of an athletic court depends on more than its finished appearance. Foundation stability, drainage, playing dimensions, and material selection all influence how the court performs over time.

Meridian's warm, dry summers, freezing winter temperatures, irrigated landscapes, and varying residential lot conditions create several important construction considerations.

- **Freeze-thaw exposure:** Moisture entering pavement cracks can freeze and expand during winter, contributing to further deterioration. Proper base preparation and drainage help address these concerns.
- **Soil stability:** Properties with clay-rich soil, disturbed fill, or poorly compacted ground may require additional preparation to support a court foundation.
- **Existing slopes and elevation changes:** Uneven backyards may need excavation, grading, or additional site improvements before court construction.
- **Irrigation and drainage:** Sprinkler systems, nearby landscaping, rainfall, and snowmelt should be considered when determining how water will move around the court.
- **Limited backyard space:** Smaller residential lots require careful planning of playing boundaries, equipment locations, and clearance around the court.
- **Summer temperature changes:** Outdoor materials should be selected with sunlight exposure, thermal movement, and seasonal temperatures in mind.
- **Deteriorating playing surfaces:** Cracks, uneven pavement, faded markings, and worn coatings may require repairs before resurfacing can take place.

Our construction approach accounts for these site-specific conditions instead of relying on the same installation method for every Meridian property.

## Comparing Athletic Court Surfaces for Your Project

Different athletic surfaces provide different playing characteristics, installation requirements, and maintenance considerations.

| Surface Material | Suitable Applications | Important Considerations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard acrylic | Outdoor pickleball, tennis, and basketball | Consistent playing characteristics; requires a suitable paved foundation |

| Asphalt | Supporting foundations for applicable outdoor court systems | Base stability, compaction, and pavement condition are important |

### Standard Acrylic vs. Cushioned Acrylic

Standard acrylic systems are widely used for outdoor racquet and basketball courts because they provide defined surface textures, customizable colors, and consistent playing characteristics.

Cushioned acrylic incorporates additional layers beneath the finished coating to change the feel of the playing surface.

Both systems depend on the quality and condition of the underlying foundation.

### When Is Modular Athletic Flooring a Good Option?

Interlocking athletic tiles can be suitable for backyard basketball courts and selected multi-use installations.

Individual tiles may be replaceable if damage occurs, depending on the flooring system. Certain outdoor modular products also allow water to pass through openings in the surface.

However, modular flooring still requires an appropriate supporting base. Product-specific ball response, traction, expansion requirements, and compatibility with the intended sport should be evaluated before installation.

### Concrete vs. Asphalt Court Foundations

Concrete and asphalt are commonly used as supporting surfaces beneath outdoor athletic coatings.

Concrete provides a rigid foundation, while asphalt offers a more flexible pavement structure. Both materials can experience deterioration when drainage, ground movement, or construction conditions are not adequately addressed.

The appropriate foundation depends on the property's soil conditions, intended surface system, construction requirements, and long-term maintenance expectations.

## Keeping Your Outdoor Athletic Court in Good Condition

Meridian's seasonal weather makes routine court care an important part of protecting an outdoor recreational investment.

General maintenance recommendations include:

- Remove leaves, dirt, and other debris regularly.
