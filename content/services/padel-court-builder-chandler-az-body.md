## Key Features That Shape a Padel Court's Playing Experience

Unlike a traditional tennis court, padel uses a smaller enclosed playing area where the surrounding walls are part of the game. This means the enclosure, court surface, and layout need to work together.

A standard padel doubles court has a playing area measuring 10 meters wide by 20 meters long. The complete project also needs appropriate space for the enclosure, access, and any surrounding features.

Court enclosure: Walls and fencing define the playing area and allow players to use rebounds during matches.

Playing surface: Padel courts commonly use synthetic turf systems designed for the sport. Surface choice can affect footing and ball movement.

Court lighting: Lighting can make the facility more practical for evening games and organized recreational activities.

Site preparation: A suitable base supports the playing surface and the rest of the court structure.
