## Maintaining Athletic Courts in Arizona's Desert Climate

Outdoor recreational courts in Chandler require appropriate care to preserve their playing characteristics and appearance.

### Remove Dust and Surface Debris

Windblown dust, leaves, and other debris can accumulate on outdoor playing areas.

Regular cleaning with equipment appropriate for the installed surface helps maintain consistent playing conditions.

Avoid cleaning methods that could damage acrylic coatings, modular materials, or other athletic flooring.

### Inspect the Court After Monsoon Storms

Heavy seasonal rainfall can reveal drainage problems that may not be noticeable during dry weather.

Look for recurring puddles, displaced materials, erosion around the court perimeter, and debris carried onto the playing surface.

Addressing these conditions early may help prevent further deterioration.

### Monitor Acrylic Coatings for Wear

Outdoor acrylic surfaces may gradually lose color and texture through regular use and continued weather exposure.

Faded lines, exposed pavement, peeling coatings, and changes in traction may indicate that resurfacing should be considered.

The appropriate resurfacing schedule depends on the surface condition rather than a fixed number of years.

### Check Modular Flooring Connections

Modular court surfaces should be inspected for loose tiles, damaged connections, irregular movement, and accumulated debris.

Maintenance should follow the manufacturer's recommendations, particularly where the system experiences significant temperature changes.

### Maintain Court Equipment and Fencing

Basketball hoops, net systems, fence gates, windscreen attachments, and other components should be checked periodically for deterioration or movement.

Lighting fixtures should also be maintained according to applicable equipment and electrical requirements.
